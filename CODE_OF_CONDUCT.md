# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

NetsuRush contributors and maintainers commit to offering everyone an open, welcoming, constructive
and harassment-free space to take part in, whatever their experience, identity, background or skill
level.

## Expected behaviour

- Communicate with respect, clarity and patience.
- Welcome constructive feedback.
- Help others learn.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Put the interest of the project and its community first.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, discrimination, intimidation or threats.
- Insults, personal attacks, trolling or repeated bad-faith interactions.
- Sexual content or unsolicited sexual attention.
- Spam or deliberate disruption of discussions.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.

## Scope

This code applies in every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions and other community
spaces tied to NetsuRush. It also applies when someone officially represents the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove any content that breaks this code, refuse a contribution, and
apply a warning, a temporary ban or a permanent ban.

To report unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainer privately from the
[NetsumaInfo GitHub profile](https://github.com/NetsumaInfo). Reports are reviewed discreetly and
confidentially.
