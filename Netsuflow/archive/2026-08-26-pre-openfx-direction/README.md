# Archived research: pre-OpenFX direction

Archived on 2026-08-26 after narrowing the product direction.

This directory preserves the original broad investigation of render, frame-service, native translation, hybrid, IR, and framework approaches. Nothing was deleted. These documents remain useful as historical evidence, but they are no longer the active specification.

The active direction is documented in [`../../README.md`](../../README.md): a thin OpenFX Generator in Fusion backed by a persistent Remotion renderer managed by NetsuRush.

`README.original.md` is the former project entry point. `SOURCES.md`, `decision/`, `research/`, and `tests/` retain their original layout.
